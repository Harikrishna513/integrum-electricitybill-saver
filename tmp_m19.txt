> The LLM must NOT be the source of truth for electricity tariffs, bill calculations, eligibility decisions, or financial savings calculations.

* Understanding bill images
* Extracting fields
* Normalizing messy text
* Understanding user questions
* Explaining deterministic results
* Conversational interaction

* Arithmetic
* Unit calculations
* Bill calculations
* Tariff application
* Historical rule selection

```text
current_reading >= previous_reading
```

```text
current_reading - previous_reading
```

against:

```text
units_consumed
```

```text
POTENTIAL_METER_READING_MISMATCH
```

```text

```


```text
Detected discrepancy
```

from:

